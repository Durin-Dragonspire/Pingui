Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Pingui (the “App”), developed and published by Durin Dragonspire (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

We respect your privacy and have designed Pingui to keep your information on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Pingui does not transmit personal information to Durin Dragonspire, and we do not store it on servers we operate. We do not receive your data through the App, and we do not create online accounts.

You do not need to give us an email address, phone number, or physical address to use Pingui. A profile name and optional PIN stay on the device if you choose to enter them.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Pingui stores what it needs to function in local SQLite storage on your device. That can include:

  • Profile names and colors you enter
  • An optional PIN hash (not the PIN itself)
  • Reminder titles, notes, schedules, groups, and related settings
  • Local history of fired, snoozed, done, and missed alerts

This locally stored information is not transmitted to us, and we do not have access to it.

Removing the App from your device will generally remove information stored locally by the App, subject to the normal behavior of your device and operating system. You can also clear Pingui’s data in system settings.

3. Data Sharing

Because we do not collect or receive personal user data through Pingui, we do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal user data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Pingui operates on devices and platforms provided by third parties, including Android and Google Play.

These third-party services may independently process certain technical, device, or usage information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control information collected independently by your device manufacturer, operating system provider, app store, or other third-party services that are not operated by Durin Dragonspire.

5. Advertising and Analytics

We do not use personal information collected through Pingui for advertising, profiling, tracking, or marketing purposes.

We do not use personal information collected through Pingui to track users across other applications or websites.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal user data on servers operated by us, we do not retain personal user data and therefore have no server-side personal user data to delete upon request.

Information stored locally by Pingui can generally be removed by clearing the App's data through your device settings or uninstalling the App.

7. Data Security

Pingui is designed with privacy in mind, and we limit data handling to what is necessary for the App to function.

Personal user information is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Durin Dragonspire, and we do not maintain a server-side database containing personal user information.

8. Children's Privacy

Pingui does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users, and it does not transmit children’s data to us.

If you believe that Pingui is handling personal information in a way that is inconsistent with this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Pingui's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page, and the “Effective Date” at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

If a future version of Pingui begins collecting, using, or sharing personal information off the device, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices.
